3.2.13 Preprocessor Support

All macro packages provide support for various preprocessors and may extend their functionality by defining macros to caption their output and/or set it in a display. Examples include TS and TE for tbl, EQ and EN for eqn, and PS and PE for pic. Another preprocessor, refer, facilitates the inclusion of bibliographic citations in a consistent format.
